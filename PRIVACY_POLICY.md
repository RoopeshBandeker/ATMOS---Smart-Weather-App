# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

## Introduction

ATMOS - Smart Weather App respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application collects, uses, and protects user information.

By using the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

ATMOS may collect the following information:

### 1. Location Information
The app may access your device location to provide accurate weather forecasts and local weather-related information.

Location data is only used while using the application and is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

### 2. Weather Search Data
Search queries such as city names or locations entered by users may be processed to provide weather forecasts and map functionality.

### 3. Device and Usage Information
Basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, and app performance data may be collected automatically for improving app stability and performance.

---

## How We Use Information

The collected information is used for:

- Providing current weather forecasts
- Delivering hourly and daily weather updates
- Displaying weather maps and environmental information
- Improving application performance and user experience
- Fixing bugs and technical issues

## Third-Party Services

ATMOS may use third-party APIs and services including:

- OpenWeather API
- Geolocation services
- Map services

These third-party services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Sharing

ATMOS does not sell, rent, or trade user personal information to third parties.

User information may only be shared if required by law or necessary for providing app functionality through trusted service providers.

## Data Security

Reasonable security measures are used to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

ATMOS is not directed toward children under the age of 13. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
